Specialized BPO Services for International Clients

Winter Shand Inc. is a Business Process Outsourcing corporation headquartered in Makati City, Philippines. We provide voice and non-voice communication services, customer support operations, and back-office processing to international corporate clients on contract.

Our Mission

Established in Makati City, Winter Shand Inc. delivers specialized audio, video, and text-based communication operations through trained, on-site agents. By combining a skilled workforce with secure, modern infrastructure, we provide international companies with reliable offshore service delivery at scale.

Core Competencies

  • Voice and non-voice customer support operations
  • English-language communication services
  • Audio and video session delivery (WebRTC infrastructure)
  • Back-office processing and data services
  • Quality assurance and on-site supervision
  • Digital payment reconciliation

Engagement Model

Winter Shand Inc. operates exclusively under contract with international corporate clients. All agent operations are conducted on-site at our Makati office under direct supervision and a documented Code of Conduct. We are approved under the Foreign Investments Act of 1991 (R.A. 7042) as an export market enterprise with a committed 60% export ratio, and we operate strictly within the activities permitted to foreign-owned corporations under Philippine law.
